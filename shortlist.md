# I've Been Everywhere - shortlist

## Top 10 picks

1. **Lake Shore Drive** - Aliotta Haynes Jeremiah (Lake Shore Drive (Chicago), street) [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/track/46MX86XQqYCZRvwPpeq4Gi)  
   A 70s soft-rock love letter to Chicago's lakefront road. Most people know it once it plays, but it rarely gets picked.
2. **One Night in Bangkok** - Murray Head (Bangkok, city) [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/track/6erBowZaW6Ur3vNOWhS2zM)  
   From the musical Chess. Huge hook, and a trip to Thailand nobody else will bring.
3. **Ventura Highway** - America, George Martin (Ventura Highway, street) [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/track/4IU1RL4BKvFyXtbTwaHAvW)  
   Breezy and instantly familiar. The California coast in three minutes.
4. **The City of New Orleans** - Arlo Guthrie (New Orleans, city) [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/track/0P6TTXbSLGrSoTNMz5kvB7)  
   A train ride from Chicago down to New Orleans. A great 'takes you somewhere' story song.
5. **Texas Sun** - Khruangbin, Leon Bridges (Texas, state) [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/track/24ntSW3QVJzR79lHAAOTaY)  
   Khruangbin and Leon Bridges. Warm and dusty, a modern pick that shows you have taste.
6. **Baker Street** - Gerry Rafferty (Baker Street (London), street) [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/track/5gOd6zDC8vhlYjqbQdJVWP)  
   The sax riff everyone knows. London's Baker Street, of Sherlock Holmes fame.
7. **Waterloo** - ABBA (Waterloo (Belgium), town) [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/track/0RzhMHIsFMbOGh0oWDvNNK)  
   ABBA's Eurovision winner, about the Belgian battlefield. Pure fun.
8. **Tokyo Drift (Fast & Furious) - From "The Fast And The Furious: Tokyo Drift" Soundtrack** - Teriyaki Boyz (Tokyo, city) [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/track/5iJfzXKvQXlYOBtEQhEmpA)  
   Tokyo Drift. Pure nostalgia, and the room will hype it.
9. **It Never Rains in Southern California** - Albert Hammond (Southern California, region) [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/track/6tunhVGD8C05MZNjSVIsjw)  
   A sneaky deep cut. Everyone half-knows the chorus.
10. **Last Train to London** - Electric Light Orchestra (London, city) [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/track/6xE6ZWzK1YDDSYzqOCoQlz)  
   ELO disco-rock. A train to London that most of the league will have forgotten they love.
